Copernicus Land Monitoring Service (CLMS)

The Copernicus Land Monitoring Service (CLMS) provides geographical information on land cover and land use and their changes, ground motion, vegetation state, water cycle and Earth’s surface energy variables for both Europe and the entire globe.

CLMS is jointly implemented by the European Environment Agency and the European Commission DG Joint Research Centre (JRC) and has been operational since 2012.

CLMS consists of several components.

Visit the website land.copernicus.eu for further information.

Disclaimer: CLMS Product Availability on CDSE

CLMS is in the process of onboarding its products to the CDSE. The migration process is expected to last until the end of 2025. Until the process is finalized, not all CLMS products will be available on the CDSE. Onboarded products will be listed in the tables below.

For the complete CLMS data catalog, please consult land.copernicus.eu.

We appreciate your understanding and patience as we work to integrate our full suite of products. For the most up-to-date information on product availability, please check back regularly or contact our support team.

Thank you for your continued support.

Data access on CDSE

CLMS products are accessible via S3 Access, OData, the Sentinel Hub APIs, and the Copernicus Browser.

For downloadable lists in CSV format, refer to the CSV Copernicus Catalogue.

To help you construct OData queries tailored to your needs, we provide example queries along with detailed information on CLMS-specific metadata parameters. See Catalogue and search attributes and CLMS OData metadata specification.

We provide product-specific S3 paths and data-access links in the overview tables below.



Land Cover and Land Use Mapping

Land Cover and Land Use Mapping produces land cover classifications at various level of detail, both within a pan-European and global context. At the pan-European level, these are complemented by detailed layers on land cover characteristics, such as imperviousness, forests, grassland, water and wetness and small woody features. At global level, the land cover mapping follows the FAO’s modular-hierarchical Land Cover Classification System.

Global Dynamic Land Cover

Overview

The Global Dynamic Land Cover product offers annual global land cover maps at 10 m and 100 m spatial resolution, as well as a pantropical tree cover density map at 10 m spatial resolution. The land cover map at 100 m spatial resolution provides a detailed view of land cover at three classification levels as well as cover fraction layers.

Bio-geophysical Parameters

The systematic monitoring of Bio-geophysical Parameters produces a series of qualified bio-geophysical products on the status and evolution of the land surface. The products are used to monitor vegetation, crops, water cycle, energy budget and terrestrial cryosphere variables.

Soil Moisture

Overview

The Soil Moisture product group provides comprehensive datasets on soil moisture, including Surface Soil Moisture, which provides information on the relative water content of the top few centimeters soil, and the Soil Water Index, which quantifies the moisture condition at various depths in the soil.

Catalogue and search attributes

All data sets in the CLMS collection can be accessed using CLMS-specific metadata parameters.
This section explains the specific attributes and the relevant allowable values that can be utilized in data searches via the OData API and the Copernicus Browser.

For further guidance on how to use OData and the Browser, kindly refer to the OData and About the Browser pages.

Examples of OData queries

Below are some (not exhaustive) examples of OData queries that you can use and modify according to your needs.
List of acceptable expand attribute names for CLMS:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Attributes(CLMS)

A description of all metadata including the additional attributes can be found in CLMS OData metadata specification.

To search the entire CLMS collection:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27

Tip

Please be aware that the results are paginated and limited to 20 products per page. The @odata.nextLink at the bottom of the response provides the link for the next 20 products.

Information for disabling this page limitation can be found in the OData Top option documentation.

To search all products in COG format of a specific dataset (e.g. wb_global_100m_monthly_v1) within the CLMS collection:

A list of all dataset identifiers is available here.

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27%20and%20Attributes/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/any(att:att/Name%20eq%20%27datasetIdentifier%27%20and%20att/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/Value%20eq%20%27wb_global_100m_monthly_v1%27)%20and%20Attributes/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/any(att:att/Name%20eq%20%27fileFormat%27%20and%20att/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/Value%20eq%20%27cog%27)&$count=true&$expand=Attributes

To search for CLMS specific product by name:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Name%20eq%20%27c_gls_SWI_202505201200_GLOBE_ASCAT_V3.2.1_nc%27&$expand=Attributes

To search for CLMS collection and products within all CLMS data sets acquired between two dates:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27%20and%20ContentDate/Start%20gt%202005-05-03T00:00:00.000Z%20and%20ContentDate/Start%20lt%202022-05-03T00:11:00.000Z

To search for CLMS collection products within all CLMS data sets with footprints intersecting specific coordinates:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27%20and%20OData.CSC.Intersects(area=geography%27SRID=4326;POLYGON((12.655118166047592%2047.44667197521409,21.39065656328509%2048.347694733853245,28.334291357162826%2041.877123516783655,17.47086198383573%2040.35854475076158,12.655118166047592%2047.44667197521409))%27)&$top=20

To search for CLMS collection products acquired between two dates with footprints intersecting specific coordinates:

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27%20and%20OData.CSC.Intersects(area=geography%27SRID=4326;POLYGON((12.655118166047592%2047.44667197521409,21.39065656328509%2048.347694733853245,28.334291357162826%2041.877123516783655,17.47086198383573%2040.35854475076158,12.655118166047592%2047.44667197521409))%27)%20and%20ContentDate/Start%20gt%202021-05-20T00:00:00.000Z%20and%20ContentDate/Start%20lt%202021-07-21T00:00:00.000Z

To search for CLMS collection products acquired for a specific tile or grid. In the example below the request will search for the C0086 tile within the swi time series collection (12,5km daily v3).

  • HTTPS Request

https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Collection/Name%20eq%20%27CLMS%27%20and%20Attributes/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/any(att:att/Name%20eq%20%27datasetIdentifier%27%20and%20att/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/Value%20eq%20%27swi-timeseries_global_12.5km_daily_v3%27)%20and%20Attributes/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/any(att:att/Name%20eq%20%27cellID%27%20and%20att/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/Value%20eq%20%27C0086%27)&$count=true&$expand=Attributes

More details on the structure of OData queries and available functionalities can be found in the OData documentation.
