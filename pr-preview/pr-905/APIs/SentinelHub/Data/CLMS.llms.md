# CLMS

## Overview

The Copernicus Land Monitoring Service (CLMS), which is part of the Copernicus programme, provides geographical information on land cover and its changes, land use, ground motion, vegetation state, water cycle and earth surface energy variables for both Europe and the entire globe. All products are free of charge and can be used for any purpose.
