## Shuttle Radar Topography Mission DEM (SRTM DEM)

[![User guide](https://img.shields.io/badge/-User_guide-77cc09.png)](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/products/srtmgl1v003/)

#### Overview

The Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) aimed to obtain elevation data on a near-global scale in order to generate the complete and high-resolution digital topographic database of Earth.
